Our Difference

Our Food Solutions Division has evolved around helping our customers in the Food Service market solve the challenges around kitchen profitability, rising consumer food expectations and maintaining their competitive advantage.

Our Customers

Our strategy is to focus on a smaller number of customers and offer them a very high level of service. We develop relationships and work together to improve their business. Our customer base is diverse and includes five star hotels, stadiums.

Our Service

We have state-based Customer Service Teams operating from our Perth and Brisbane facilities, who can be contacted by phone or email for product advice and delivery enquiries.We also supply product through purchasing platforms.

Our Partners

Our R&D team of experienced chefs partner with the best manufacturers in the world to produce our range of premium, value-added products.We also exclusively represent a select range of premium brands including ice cream and Chobani yoghurt.

Our Brands

Our brands are synonymous with quality and encompass an extensive range of value-added products that offer both convenience and reliability. Although the range is constantly evolving and leading the way in innovation.

Our Team
